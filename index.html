05METHOD & PROCESS

From workbook
to race story.

A repeatable workflow designed to make the analysis transparent, traceable, and clear about its limitations.

  1. 01
    EXCEL

    Collect the data

    Review the source workbook and record its tables, fields, and provenance.

  2. 02
    QUALITY CHECK

    Inspect & validate

    Check coverage, missing values, duplicates, types, and definitions before analysis.

  3. 03
    PYTHON · PANDAS

    Prepare in Colab

    Clean, normalize, and transform data with repeatable notebook steps.

  4. 04
    CSV

    Export clean tables

    Write analysis-ready extracts with consistent fields and documented assumptions.

  5. 05
    TABLEAU PUBLIC

    Build visualizations

    Design focused views for performance, reliability, circuits, and strategy.

  6. 06
    DASHBOARD · STORY

    Connect the story

    Bring related views together with deliberate filters and explanatory context.

  7. 07
    QA

    Test performance

    Verify calculations, filter behavior, legibility, and dashboard responsiveness.

  8. 08
    PUBLISH

    Document & publish

    Publish the validated work and document scope, caveats, and update status.

08ABOUT THE PROJECT

Turning race data
into understanding.

This academic project uses data analysis and visualization to explore patterns across Formula 1 racing from 2010–2026. Its aim is to make complex records easier to inspect, compare, and discuss—not to present an official or live view of the sport.

The workbook describes five source tables covering race results, championship standings, circuits, lap intelligence, and qualifying. Table definitions alone do not verify completeness or historical accuracy; those limitations must be checked before publishing any finding.
